01 — GETTING ONLINE

SIM, ISP aur Broadband ke basics

Jab tum "internet chalate ho," asal mein tum ek real company se, real infrastructure use karne ka access khareed rahe ho.

sim_card
Chhota chip jo phone ko telecom company ke paas identify karta hai — ek ID card ki tarah.
isp
Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL — jo towers/cables ke maalik hain aur internet access bechte hain.
mobile_tower
Woh tall antenna structures — phone inse radio waves ke through baat karta hai.
data
Actual info jo travel karti hai — webpage, video, message. Measured in MB/GB.
recharge
ISP ko payment — fixed data + calling, fixed dino ke liye valid.
broadband
Ghar tak seedha wired (cable/fiber) connection — mobile tower ke bina.
router_modem
Broadband signal ko WiFi mein convert karne wala box, blinking lights ke saath.
wifi
Router se wireless connection — "connected but no internet" bhi possible hai!
02 — SCALE OF CONNECTIONS

Network ke 4 Types

Network matlab do ya zyada devices jo aapas mein baat kar sakte hain. Size ke hisaab se 4 categories.

WAN
MAN
LAN
📱
PAN
Personal Area Network

Kuch meters ka range — phone se Bluetooth earbuds ya smartwatch.

LAN
Local Area Network

Ek ghar, classroom, ya office floor — jaise home WiFi ke sab devices.

MAN
Metropolitan Area Network

Poore city ya bade campus ki multiple LANs — jaise university ke sab departments.

WAN
Wide Area Network

Cities, states, countries ya poori duniya — ISP ka nationwide backbone.

06 — ADDRESS FORMATS

IPv4 vs IPv6

Duniya mein devices itni badh gayi ki purana format khatam hone laga — isliye naya format aaya.

IPv4

142.250.183.78
  • 4 numbers (0–255), dots se separated
  • 32-bit — total ~4.3 billion unique addresses
  • Purana, ab addresses khatam ho rahe hain

IPv6

2401:4900:1c20:abcd:0000:0000:0000:1a2b
  • Hexadecimal + colons se banta hai
  • 128-bit — undecillion addresses possible
  • Naya format, shortage solve karta hai
